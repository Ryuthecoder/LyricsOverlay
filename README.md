# Project Brief
## Problem: Within gamers who enjoy listening to music while engaging in gaming, there are groups of gamers who enjoy singing to the music. Yet, many struggle to make this plausible when there is a lack of lyric overlays that are passed by the anticheat system, and external lyric monitors like second monitor or mini display require extra cost for a mere lyric use. Therefore, a need for an accessible external lyric display is high.

## Criteria: 

###Software: 

	- The program must show lyrics that are synced with the Spotify player at a reliable (consistent connectivity) pace. 
	- The program must require little to minimal steps to set up the program. 
		- It must be easy for users who do not have experience with programming to set up.
- It must not breach the TOS of Spotify. 
- The program supports many lyrics from different types of music to match the diverse music taste of the gamers. 

###Hardware: 

- The finished external display needs to offer a design that is distraction-less and reliable connection to live lyrics:
	- A rectangular minimalistic design that has smooth corners
	- Colors of similarly range
	- A display module that is easy to see:
		- At least 1 line of upcoming or up-next lyrics is fit or shown on the screen.
		
##Constraints: 

###Software:

- The program must rely on free APIs to ensure the longevity of the program. 
	- Minimal funds are provided by the school.
	- Since the project involves a hardware aspect, time allocation for software development must be decided accordingly.
	
###Hardware:

	- The manufacturing of external display needs to be accessible and relatively cheap:
		- The rectangular frame will be 3D-printed using school’s 3D printer:
		- Filament: Matte PLA, an accessible and cheap filament that offers 
non-reflective and smooth surface.
	- The size of the display is suitable for placing it on the desktop.
